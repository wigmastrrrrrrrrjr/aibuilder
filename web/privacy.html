Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-09

This Privacy Policy explains what aibuilder ("we", "our", or the "Service") collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. What We Collect

  • Account. A username and a password, stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash (your plaintext password is never kept or shown back to you). We do not collect your email address.
  • Date of birth. Asked only to enforce the 13+ (COPPA) age gate at signup. It is validated when you sign up and is never stored.
  • Network fingerprint. We record a keyed hash (HMAC) of the network address used at signup, so we can enforce duplicate-account and anti-abuse limits. We cannot reverse the hash back into your address.
  • Your content. The apps you build, files you upload, chat and build instructions you send, project settings, and anything you publish to the discovery feed.
  • Usage. Session tokens (to keep you signed in), live-room activity when you use collaboration/Realtime features, and credit ledger entries.

2. How We Use It

We use this data to operate the Service: run and save your projects, stream AI responses, publish apps you choose to share, enforce rate limits and abuse protections, and improve performance. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your content to train third-party models.

3. Where Your Data Lives

The Service runs on third-party cloud infrastructure. Your account data and content are stored in a Cloudflare Workers/D1 database and, for newer real-time features, a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database. Data is encrypted in transit over HTTPS and protected at rest by these providers' safeguards plus our own hashing of sensitive account fields described above.

4. What We Share

We share data only with (a) the infrastructure providers that host the Service, (b) the third-party models we call to generate output, which receive the text you send them, and (c) other users, and only for content you deliberately publish or share — such as published projects, live rooms, or team invites. We do not sell your information.

5. Retention

We keep your account and content while your account is active. Deleting your account removes your projects, files, and personal data from the active databases. Abuse-related records may be retained longer where needed to prevent further abuse.

6. Your Choices

  • Sign out / stop using — your session ends immediately.
  • Delete your account — removes your data as described in Section 5.
  • Published content — unpublish or delete any project to remove it from the discovery feed.

7. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the latest version, and material changes will be noted on this page.

9. Contact

Questions about this Policy? Reach us through the account menu in the Service.
